# Content Skill

Use for briefs, reviews, comparisons, tutorials, guides, and evergreen content.

## Required structure
- Search intent
- Primary topic/keyword
- Clear answer near the top
- Helpful headings and examples
- Natural internal links
- Practical next steps or CTA when useful
- Metadata suggestions
- Update date when freshness matters

## Review content
Use: what was evaluated, features, pricing, ease of use, output quality, pros/cons, best for, limitations, alternatives, last updated/tested status, affiliate disclosure.

## Guardrails
- Never invent testing, credentials, statistics, quotes, customers, or product capabilities.
- Separate verified facts from inference.
- Prioritize usefulness over word count.
