# Keyword Research Skill

Use for deciding what to publish and what to prioritize.

## Required inputs
Prefer actual data from Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Search Console, or supplied research.

## Evaluate
- Search volume
- Keyword difficulty
- Search intent
- SERP strength/quality
- Traffic potential when available
- Commercial value
- Topical fit

## Classification
- Best opportunity
- Good opportunity
- Later
- Reject

## Guardrails
- Claude estimates are hypotheses until validated.
- Low volume is not automatically bad if intent and competition are favorable.
- High volume is not automatically good if the SERP is dominated by very strong sites.
