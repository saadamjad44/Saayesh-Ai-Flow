**Claude Duet is an Anthropic app in which Claude stops waiting to be asked.** It connects to the apps you already work in, sends you a short briefing each morning of what needs attention, checks those accounts through the day, and messages you when something needs a decision from you. Tasks you hand it continue in the background on its own virtual machine, so they keep running when your laptop is closed.

Anthropic previewed it on 21 October 2026, built on Claude Opus 5.5, and describes the intended user as professionals and small business owners — which makes it the only product in this new category aimed squarely at people running something rather than people running errands.

This page is what Duet is and how it works. If you are weighing it against the two rival products that shipped weeks earlier, [Claude Duet vs OpenAI Dots vs Meta Muse](/ai-agents/claude-duet-vs-openai-dots-vs-meta-muse/) is the comparison, and it starts with who is eligible for what.

## What "proactive" actually means here

The word is doing real work in this case, so it is worth being precise about it.

Ordinary Claude is a tool you go to. You open it, ask for something, get it, and the thread ends when you leave. Everything useful that happens is downstream of you remembering to start.

Duet inverts that. The work it does falls into four modes:

That third row is the one to weigh. A product whose job is deciding what deserves your attention is only as good as that decision, and the failure mode of getting it wrong is not a visible error. It is either noise you learn to ignore, or silence about something that mattered.

## What it connects to, and what it can build

An agent is only as capable as its access, and Duet's access is unusually broad: Anthropic puts the Claude marketplace at over 6,000 connectors and plug-ins.

The reported integrations are notable less for their number than for their shape. **Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, Gusto, LinkedIn, DocuSign, HubSpot, Canva and Shopify** is not a consumer list. It is mail, chat, books, payments, payroll, contracts, CRM, design and storefront — which is, roughly, the software stack of a small business.

The work it does with that access spans a wider range than the briefing suggests:

- **Administrative:** scheduling meetings, handling the back-and-forth, triaging what arrived.
- **Analytical:** building dashboards and putting together forecasts from connected data.
- **Generative:** prototyping small web apps.
- **Transactional:** making a purchase through Stripe Link or Shop Pay — with your approval, never alone.

## The cost nobody mentions

During the preview Duet is not a separate subscription — it comes with Claude Pro or Max. But it is not free in the way that sounds.

**Duet shares your plan's usage limits with the rest of Claude, Claude Code included.** The checks it runs on your behalf through the day draw on the same allowance your own work uses. That is why it checks every few hours rather than continuously, and it is the practical fact most launch coverage leaves out.

So the real question is not whether Duet costs extra. It is whether you have headroom. If you already bump against your Pro limits by Thursday, a background agent sharing that budget will be felt. If you use Claude lightly, you will not notice.
