Not sure which AI tool to try first? This quiz narrows it down in under a minute.

Answer four questions about what you need, what you can spend, how much experience you have, and what matters most to you. The quiz scores every tool this site has reviewed against your answers and shows the three best matches, with honest trade-offs and a link to the full guide for each one.

No sign-up. No email. No data leaves your browser.

## How the scoring works

Every tool in the quiz carries a set of scores — one for each possible answer across the four questions. When you pick "Writing blog posts, emails, or marketing copy" as your use case, tools that are strong at writing get more points; when you pick "Free only", tools with a capable free tier jump ahead.

Your four answers produce a total score for each tool. The highest scorer is your top recommendation; the next two are your alternatives.

The weights come from our own research into each tool — published guides on this site that you can read and verify. No tool is boosted because of an affiliate deal, and the scoring logic is [open in the source code](https://github.com/saadamjad44/SAF/blob/main/src/components/tools/tool-finder-quiz.ts) for anyone who wants to check.

## What the quiz does not do

It does not test the tools for you. The recommendations are based on published capabilities, pricing, and our research — not on automated benchmarks or live API calls. For hands-on comparisons, follow the guide links in your results.

It also does not cover every AI tool that exists. It covers the ones this site has reviewed, which means the catalogue is small but every recommendation links to a guide you can actually read.
