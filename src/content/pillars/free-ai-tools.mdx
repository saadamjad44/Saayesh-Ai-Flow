This section is different from the rest of the site. The guides elsewhere help you understand and choose AI tools. The tools here let you act on that understanding — find the right tool for your situation, grab a prompt you can use right now, or see what launched this week.

Everything here is free, runs in your browser, and collects nothing. No sign-up, no email gate, no trial that expires.

## What is here

**[AI Tool Finder Quiz](/free-ai-tools/ai-tool-finder-quiz/)** — Answer four questions and get a personalised recommendation. The quiz draws from the tools this site has reviewed, states the trade-offs honestly, and links to the full guide so you can verify the recommendation yourself.

**[Prompt Library](/free-ai-tools/prompt-library/)** — Ready-made prompts for writing, marketing, business operations, and more. Search by task, copy with one click, and follow the link to the guide that covers the subject. Each prompt works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any general-purpose AI tool.

**[AI Weekly Radar](/free-ai-tools/ai-weekly-radar/)** — What launched, what changed, and what broke in AI tools this week. A short, skimmable digest for people who want to stay current without reading ten newsletters.

## Why we built these

Most AI tool websites stop at articles. That is useful, but it means a reader has to do the matching, the collecting, and the keeping-up themselves. These tools close that gap — the quiz does the matching, the library does the collecting, and the radar does the keeping-up.

If you are new to AI tools and do not know where to start, the quiz is the right first step. If you already use a tool and want better prompts for it, the library is where to go. And if you want to know whether something new is worth switching to, the radar covers that every week.
